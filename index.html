Pebbloto

Control your Yoto from your Pebble

A free remote control for Yoto players on the Pebble Time 2: browse your library, pick a track, play, pause and change the volume from your wrist. Works with Yoto.

Privacy policy

Pebbloto is an independent, third-party app that uses the Yoto API. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Yoto. Contact: @rosewarne
